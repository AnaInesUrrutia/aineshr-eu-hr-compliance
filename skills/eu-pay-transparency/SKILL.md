# EU Pay Transparency Directive readiness

Help employers prepare for the Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970. The directive must be transposed into national law, so requirements differ by country. Before answering, search the web for the national transposition status of the countries involved and cite sources. Say clearly when a country has not yet transposed it.

## Core requirements to cover

1. **Before employment:** applicants get information on the initial pay or pay range and, where relevant, collective agreement provisions. Employers may not ask about pay history.
2. **Gender-neutral job titles and recruitment criteria.**
3. **Pay-setting transparency:** employers must make available the objective, gender-neutral criteria used to set pay and pay progression.
4. **Right to information:** employees can request their individual pay level and the average pay levels for categories of workers doing equal or equal-value work, broken down by sex.
5. **No pay secrecy clauses:** employees may disclose their pay to enforce equal pay.
6. **Gender pay gap reporting:** larger employers report on a schedule set by headcount. Verify the thresholds and first reporting dates for each country.
7. **Joint pay assessment:** if reporting shows an unexplained average gap of 5% or more in a category of workers, and it is not justified by objective criteria, the employer must carry out a joint pay assessment with worker representatives.
8. **Remedies:** compensation for pay discrimination, and a shifted burden of proof in many cases.

## Readiness plan

Produce a table with these workstreams, owner, and next step:

- **Job architecture:** define job levels and groups of equal or equal-value work with objective criteria (skills, effort, responsibility, working conditions)
- **Pay structures:** pay ranges per level, documented progression rules
- **Data:** clean HR and payroll data, consistent pay components, sex data, mapping of employees to job groups
- **Analysis:** gender pay gap by category, with explanations
- **Process:** a repeatable way to answer employee information requests within the legal time limit
- **Recruitment:** job ad templates with pay ranges, interview guides without salary history questions
- **Communication:** manager training and employee FAQ
- **Governance:** involve works council or worker representatives early

## Output

Return: country-by-country status (with sources), a gap list against the requirements above, and a 90-day plan. Remind the user this is general information and that legal counsel should confirm national rules.
