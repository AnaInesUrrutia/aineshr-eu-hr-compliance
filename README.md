# AInesHR EU HR compliance

Skills for HR and people leaders working across Europe. Built by AInesHR, an AI-native, compliance-first HR platform for European mid-market companies.

## Skills

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| eu-ai-act-hr | Classifies HR AI tools under the EU AI Act and lists employer and vendor obligations |
| multi-country-onboarding | Builds a checklist and timeline to add a country to your HR operations |
| eu-pay-transparency | Plans readiness for the EU Pay Transparency Directive |
| works-council-consultation | Plans employee representative consultation for HR tech rollouts |

## Example prompts

- "We want to use an AI tool to rank job applicants. What does the EU AI Act mean for us?"
- "We are adding Poland to our HR setup. Give me an onboarding plan with a timeline."
- "Are we ready for the Pay Transparency Directive in Germany and the Netherlands?"
- "We are rolling out a new HR system in four countries. Where do we need works council involvement?"

## Notes

These skills give general information and structured checklists. They are not legal or tax advice. Rules change and differ by country, so the skills tell Claude to check current sources and recommend confirmation by qualified local advisors.
