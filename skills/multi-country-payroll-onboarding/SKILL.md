# Multi-country payroll onboarding

Create a practical onboarding plan for adding a country to a payroll landscape. Always tell the user that statutory details differ by country and change often, so local rules must be verified with a local payroll provider or advisor. Search the web for current rates and deadlines before quoting any number.

## Gather first

Ask only what is missing:
- Country and legal entity (existing subsidiary, new entity, or employer of record)
- Headcount and expected hiring pace
- Current payroll model (in-house, local provider, global provider)
- HRIS or ERP in use (for example Dynamics 365 Human Resources, SAP, Workday)
- Go-live date and first pay date

## Checklist structure

Produce a table with owner, deadline and status for each item.

**1. Legal and registration**
- Employer registration with tax authority
- Social security and health insurance registration
- Pension scheme registration where mandatory
- Occupational accident insurance where required
- Bank accounts and payment formats (for example SEPA)

**2. Statutory configuration**
- Income tax method and tax codes
- Employer and employee social contributions, ceilings and caps
- Statutory leave, sick pay and public holidays
- Minimum wage and collective agreements (sector or company level)
- 13th or 14th salary, holiday pay, thresholds and special rules
- Benefits in kind and their taxation
- Notice periods, severance and final pay rules

**3. Employee data and documents**
- Required personal and tax identifiers
- Contract and payslip language and content requirements
- Retention periods for payroll records
- GDPR: lawful basis, processor agreements, cross-border transfer mechanism, privacy notice

**4. Process and calendar**
- Payroll calendar: input cut-off, calculation, approval, payment, filing
- Reporting and filing: monthly, quarterly, year-end
- Approval workflow and segregation of duties
- Error correction and retro-calculation process

**5. Integration and testing**
- Data flow from HRIS to payroll to finance (GL posting)
- Parallel run: compare at least one cycle against the previous method
- Sign-off by local payroll expert and finance

**6. Go-live and hypercare**
- Communication to employees about the first payslip
- Support channel for questions
- Review after first two cycles

## Output

Give: (1) the checklist table, (2) a simple timeline working back from the first pay date, (3) top five risks for that country, (4) questions to verify with a local expert. Do not invent tax rates or contribution percentages. If numbers are needed, search and cite official sources.
